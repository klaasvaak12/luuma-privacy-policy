This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). ## Information We Collect ### Information You Provide - **Wardrobe Data**: Photos of your garments, brand information, purchase details, and notes about your clothing items - **Camera Permission**: We request camera access to allow you to photograph your garments for scanning and cataloguing - **Photo Storage**: Photos you take are stored locally on your device and, if you choose to sync, in our secure cloud database - **Photo Processing**: Photos are processed using AI services (Google Gemini) to extract garment information such as brand, materials, and care instructions ## How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: - **Provide Core Services**: Catalogue your wardrobe, track wear frequency, calculate sustainability metrics, and suggest outfits - **Improve the App**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs - **Personalization**: Provide personalized recommendations based on your wardrobe and preferences - **Weather-Based Suggestions**: Use your location to provide weather-appropriate outfit recommendations - **AI Processing**: Analyze garment photos and care labels using Google Gemini AI to extract information ## Data Storage and Security ### Cloud Storage - Your data is stored securely using **Supabase**, a cloud database service - All data is encrypted in transit and at rest - We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information ### Local Storage - Some data may be stored locally on your device for offline access - You can delete local data by uninstalling the App ### Data Retention - We retain your data for as long as you use the App - You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us ## Third-Party Services ### Supabase - Location data is used only for weather-based recommendations and is not shared with third parties ## Your Rights and Choices You have the right to: - **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data -
